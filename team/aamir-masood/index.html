Air Marshal (R) Aamir Masood

Founder & CEO · Former Deputy Chief of the Air Staff, PAF

Role
Founder & CEO
Service
Pakistan Air Force, 40 years
Honours
Hilal-i-Imtiaz (M), Sitara-e-Imtiaz (M), Tamgha-e-Imtiaz (M), Sitara-e-Basalat

Career

Air Marshal (R) Aamir Masood served the Pakistan Air Force for more than four decades, excelling at every tier of command before retiring at the most senior rank as Deputy Chief of the Air Staff, Air Defence (DCAS AD). In that role he was responsible for organising, training and equipping more than 1,500 officers, airmen and civilian personnel in the Air Defence branch, and served as Principal Staff Officer and adviser to the Chief of the Air Staff.

Commissioned as a fighter pilot in 1988, he flew a range of fighter aircraft in the PAF inventory and is a graduate of the Combat Commanders’ School. Across a career of more than 35 years he held a series of prestigious command and staff appointments, including Instructor Pilot, Squadron Commander of an F-16 Fighting Falcon squadron, Officer Commanding an F-16 Flying Wing, Base Commander of an operational base and Air Officer Commanding, Central Air Command. His senior staff roles at Air Headquarters, Islamabad, included Deputy Chief of the Air Staff (Support) and Deputy Chief of the Air Staff (Training).

He was among the founders of the Aerospace Centre of Excellence (ACE) — home to the Combat Commanders’ School, the school for standardisation of operational publications, the aggressor squadron and the Air Warfare School, which hosts national and international composite operations and exercises along with their analysis. His speciality is counter-terrorism and counter-insurgency operations, for which he received a gallantry award.

Honours & leadership

In recognition of his distinguished service he was awarded the Hilal-i-Imtiaz (Military), Sitara-e-Imtiaz (Military), Tamgha-e-Imtiaz (Military) and Sitara-e-Basalat, along with Chief of the Air Staff Commendation Certificates and a Professional Excellence Badge. He has logged 3,500 flying hours.

Beyond his official appointments, he led the PAF think tank, the Centre for Aerospace and Security Studies (CASS), as Deputy President and supervised the PAF Centre for Artificial Intelligence (CENTAIC) as chairman. He has also served as Chairman of the Royal Aeronautical Society (Islamabad Branch) and Senior Vice President of the Squash Federation of Pakistan. A keen polo player, he was President of the PAF Polo Association until his retirement.

At Omer-e-Rawan

As Founder & CEO, he now leads Omer-e-Rawan Foundation’s mission to improve the mental and physical well-being of school children through healing, healthcare and hope.
