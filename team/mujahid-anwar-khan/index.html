Air Chief Marshal (R) Mujahid Anwar Khan

Former Chief of the Air Staff, Pakistan Air Force

Honours
Nishan-i-Imtiaz (Civil & Military), Hilal-i-Imtiaz (M), Sitara-i-Imtiaz (M), Tamgha-i-Imtiaz (M)
Chief of the Air Staff
2018 – 2021
Flying hours
3,000+

Early career

Born on 23 December 1962, Air Chief Marshal Mujahid Anwar Khan was commissioned in the GD(P) Branch of the Pakistan Air Force on 23 December 1983. At the PAF Academy Asghar Khan he won the Sword of Honour, the Best Pilot Trophy and the Chairman Joint Chiefs of Staff Committee Gold Medal. He is a graduate of the PAF Air War College and holds an MSc in War Studies and Defence Management.

After completing his fighter and operational conversion courses he flew as a combat pilot with several frontline fighter squadrons. He is a Qualified Flying Instructor and a graduate of the PAF Combat Commanders’ School.

Command & staff appointments

He commanded the elite No 11 Fighter Squadron, No 36 Flying Wing and two F-16 operational bases — Mushaf and Shahbaz — a rare distinction. He went on to serve as Air Officer Commanding, Central Air Command, overseeing the operations and administration of four operational bases.

At Air Headquarters he served as Deputy Director Operations (Tactical Operations), Personal Staff Officer to two Chiefs of the Air Staff, Assistant Chief of the Air Staff (Operations), Deputy Chief of the Air Staff (Operations), Director General C4I, Director General Air Force Strategic Command and Deputy Chief of the Air Staff (Support). As an Air Marshal he held two Principal Staff Officer appointments simultaneously until assuming command of the PAF.

His professional training includes the Flying Instructors’ Course, Combat Commanders’ Course, Air Command & Staff Course (Jordan), Command Management Course, National Security & War Course, and the Senior Executive National and International Security Course at Harvard University, USA. He has more than 3,000 flying hours on aircraft including the F-16, F-6, FT-5, T-37 and MFI-17.

Chief of the Air Staff

He served as Chief of the Air Staff from 18 March 2018 to 19 March 2021. The President of Pakistan conferred the Nishan-i-Imtiaz on him for the response during Operation Swift Retort on 27 February 2019. His decorations include the Nishan-i-Imtiaz (Civil), Nishan-i-Imtiaz (Military), Hilal-i-Imtiaz (Military), Sitara-i-Imtiaz (Military) and Tamgha-i-Imtiaz (Military). He also received the Legion of Merit of the Turkish Armed Forces and the King Abdul Aziz Medal of Excellence, awarded by HRH Crown Prince Muhammad Bin Salman, for strengthening defence cooperation between the Royal Saudi Air Force and the PAF.

He is a Fellow of the Royal Aeronautical Society, chaired its Islamabad Chapter from 2016 to 2018, and served as President of the Pakistan Squash Federation from 2018 to 2021.

Family

He married Mrs Tazeen Mujahid in 1987; she holds a Master’s in Nutrition from Home Economics College, Lahore, and is a qualified nutritionist and passionate educationist. They have a son, a qualified financial analyst, and a daughter, a dentist. Both are keen golfers and swimmers.

Lt General Azhar Rashid (Retd), HI(M) Prof. Dr. Mowadat Hussain Rana
