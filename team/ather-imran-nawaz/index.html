Ather Imran Nawaz

CEO, Sybrid & ICE Animations (Lakson Group)

Role
CEO & Director, Sybrid and ICE Animations
Education
BS Computer Science (GIKI, Gold Medal), MS Computer Science (Purdue)
Boards
OPEN Islamabad, Pakistan Children’s Heart Foundation, IMSciences, Pak-Austria Fachhochschule

Leadership

Ather Imran Nawaz is Chief Executive Officer and Director of Sybrid Private Limited and ICE Animations Private Limited, both part of Pakistan’s Lakson Group. Sybrid is one of Pakistan’s largest technology solutions providers, with subsidiaries in the USA, UAE and Australia, offering software products and services, digital transformation, performance marketing, healthcare revenue-cycle management, customer support and managed services worldwide. With offices in Karachi, Islamabad, Lahore, Peshawar, Khairpur and abroad, the two companies employ more than 2,000 professionals. As CEO he oversees operations, business and finance, reporting to the Chairman of the Board.

He has served on the boards of educational and philanthropic organisations for over a decade. He was founding Chairman of the Board of OPEN Islamabad, its President from 2016 to 2018, and a member of the OPEN Global Board — the largest global network of Pakistani-origin professionals and entrepreneurs — and continues to serve on the OPEN Islamabad Board of Trustees.

Education & research

He holds a Bachelor’s in Computer Science from the Ghulam Ishaq Khan Institute (GIKI), where he won the Gold Medal for academic excellence in 1999, and a Master’s in Computer Science from Purdue University. He is an honorary member of the Upsilon Pi Epsilon IEEE Computer Science Honor Society (2003) and a Certified ScrumMaster (Virginia, 2006). His research on Agile practices in disaster management and business continuity was presented at the IBM CASCON Conference in Toronto in 2009.

Public service

He sits on the board of the Pakistan Children’s Heart Foundation, which is building Pakistan’s first paediatric cardiology hospital, and on the Boards of Governors of the Institute of Management Sciences, Peshawar, and Pak-Austria Fachhochschule, Haripur. He has contributed to the Higher Education Commission through the National Scholarship Management Committee and the Steering Committee for Improving ORIC Functions in Universities, and currently serves on NUST’s Research & Innovation Committee. He regularly lectures at institutions including the National Defence University and the Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission.
