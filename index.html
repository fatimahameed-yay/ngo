Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the Omer-e-Rawan Health & Wellness Centre initiative.

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Talk to our team.

What is the Omer-e-Rawan Health & Wellness Centre initiative?

It is a programme that brings integrated, comprehensive healthcare into educational institutions for both students and faculty. It focuses on the physical and mental health challenges that can hold back a young person’s growth and development.

Why is this initiative important?

Many students struggle with physical, psychological and mental health concerns — from vision problems and behavioural difficulties to emotional stress and anxiety — that directly affect how they learn and develop. Pakistan currently has very few mechanisms to identify and address these issues, so the Omer-e-Rawan Centre was launched to offer holistic support through screening, counselling, referrals and wellness services for students and faculty.

What are the key objectives of the Omer-e-Rawan Centre?

To build awareness of physical and mental well-being, to provide confidential care, and to make referrals for specialised treatment simple and fast.

What activities does the Centre undertake?

The programme takes a multi-faceted approach to student and teacher health. Activities include school consultations, developing an activity calendar, setting KPIs, audits, an MoU with the Ministry of Education, a website and media campaign, feedback mechanisms, collaboration with medical institutions, master-trainer training, health screenings and telemedicine consultations. A central focus is raising awareness and removing the stigma around physical and mental health among parents, teachers and students, with an emphasis on early intervention to prevent chronic illness. Data collected throughout allows the impact to be measured and reported.
