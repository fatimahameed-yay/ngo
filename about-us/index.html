About Omer-e-Rawan Foundation

A not-for-profit dedicated to holistic health and wellness — offering early intervention, accessible care and training to support students and educators.

Healthier schools, stronger communities

Omer-e-Rawan Foundation is a not-for-profit company set up under Section 42 of the Companies Act 2017, Pakistan.

We promote holistic health and wellness in education. Working inside schools, we provide early intervention, accessible care and professional training so that students and educators have the support they need — physically and mentally.

Section 42 non-profit CUIN 0282893 NTN G028182 Islamabad, Pakistan

Our mission

To nurture healthier minds, healthier bodies and stronger communities.

We work to improve the quality of life of children and communities by promoting mental well-being, physical health, education and social inclusion. Through prevention, awareness, early support and accessible care, we aim to build environments where every child feels supported, stays healthy and thrives.

Our vision

A future where every child has the chance to be healthy, supported and empowered to thrive.

We envision a Pakistan where every child — whatever their background or circumstances — has equitable access to quality healthcare and emotional support; where mental and physical health are valued equally, help is available without stigma, and every child receives the care they need to grow, learn and reach their full potential.

A holistic approach to well-being

01

Raising awareness

Helping students, parents and teachers understand why physical and mental health matter so much to learning.

02

Early detection

Spotting health concerns early and responding with evidence-based medical and psychosocial care.

03

Confidentiality

Care delivered by trained professionals, with every student’s privacy protected.

04

Streamlined support

Fast access to specialist referrals through telemedicine and in-person consultations.
