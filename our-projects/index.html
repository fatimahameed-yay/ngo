Our projects

We remove health barriers in education through comprehensive, community-driven programmes — from early screening to faculty training and awareness campaigns — focused on the whole well-being of students and educators.

School-Based Wellness Initiative

Six connected programmes that take a student from awareness, to early detection, to the right care.

ASPIRE Student Well-being Survey

Delivered across 9 schools, the ASPIRE survey engaged and screened 3,572 students to measure key well-being indicators and help schools build more supportive environments. Omer-e-Rawan Foundation also served as a consultant organisation alongside NUST in developing the ASPIRE national policy framework.

OeR Health & Wellness Centre

Our flagship school-based model. Each centre brings in-school screening, wellness programmes, counselling, telemedicine support and referrals together under one roof, so students and faculty get timely care without leaving campus.

Awareness & Training Programmes
