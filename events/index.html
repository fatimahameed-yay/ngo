OeR Awareness & Fundraising Tour, USA

June – July 2026New York · Washington DC · Dallas · Houston

Join us for an evening of authentic Pakistani cuisine and the chance to meet Air Marshal (R) Aamir Masood — a decorated Pakistan Air Force F-16 fighter pilot, commander, and the Founder & CEO of Omer-e-Rawan Foundation. Hear how a distinguished 40-year military career led him to champion the mental and physical well-being of school children through healing, healthcare and hope.

Dinner was complimentary at our New York and Dallas events, and contributions to the Foundation’s mission were welcome but entirely voluntary. Each evening brought together humanitarians and community leaders committed to improving children’s lives through health, education and opportunity.
